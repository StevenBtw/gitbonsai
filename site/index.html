Grow a bonsai from your GitHub activity.

An animated SVG for a profile README. A newcomer is a thin sprout; years of work make it short, thick and wide, the way a prize bonsai is. Each language claims a strand of the trunk, and nothing is ever erased.

Reading a tree

What every part shows

The main signs first. Open a card for the rest, or see each one on a tree above.

What it's made of

The trunk
Your first language; it only grows
Leaf color
The language, on the Japanese maple palette
Strands
Each language with at least 10% of your work; it comes away from the trunk the more you use it
Silver leaves
Private work, when your profile shares it; their tips take your public languages' colors
A white trunk
A classic language such as COBOL or Fortran, still growing: the rarest shape's honor
See three languages' leaves on @StevenBtw

Time and growth

Girth
Every contribution ever, and your age
The crown
Your last three to six years, a sector per language with a real share of them
Fullness
How busy your recent years were; old leaves thin out
Low limbs
Earlier eras, oldest lowest
Bronze tips
New growth from last week
See 16 years of girth on @torvalds

Pauses and changes

Bleached deadwood
A language you stopped using while you kept working; it dies in place and weathers over the years
A bare tree
A year without contributions: resting for the winter
A gap of bare trunk
A long pause before your recent years
A bleached strip
The language your tree started with, dropped

Add it to your profile

Three steps, then it grows every week

Everything runs in your own profile repository (you/you) with GitHub's built-in token.

you/you

you/you / .github/workflows/bonsai.yml

name: bonsaion:  schedule:    - cron: "17 6 * * 1"  workflow_dispatch:permissions:  contents: writejobs:  bonsai:    runs-on: ubuntu-latest    steps:      - uses: actions/checkout@v7      - uses: StevenBtw/gitbonsai@v@VERSION@        with:          output-dir: assets      - name: Commit if it changed        run: |          git config user.name "github-actions[bot]"          git config user.email "41898282+github-actions[bot]@users.noreply.github.com"          git add assets          git diff --cached --quiet || git commit -m "Grow bonsai"          git push

you/you / Actions

bonsai · run workflow
Grow bonsai · github-actions[bot]
